See the Colibri at your shop

The Colibri is a drive-through scanner arch that maps hail and dent damage in minutes. Find your shop, see the arch life-size with your phone, and send us a few photos. We'll tell you where it fits best.

1

Find your shop

Open Street View

Tap the map to move the blue outline. It shows the arch plus the drive-through lane, to scale.

2

See it life-size

On a phone, tap “View in your shop”, stand back about 25 ft from where it should go, and point at open floor.

Sizes are approximate. In camera view the arch appears just in front of where you point, at full size, so you may need to step back or look up to see all of it. The light-blue strip on the floor is the lane a vehicle drives through during a scan.

Send us your space

Add a few photos of the bay or lot where the scanner would go: one from each end and one from the side helps most.
